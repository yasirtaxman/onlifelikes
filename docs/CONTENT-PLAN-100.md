# On Life Likes — First 100 Article Plan

Mix: **30 Amazon buyer guides · 30 informational (AdSense) articles · 20 comparisons · 20 best-under-budget.**
Priority order: publish the six clusters below first (30 articles), then the backlog tables. Target cadence: 1/day.

Conventions used below:
- **Intent** — `commercial` (buyer research), `informational` (learning), `comparison` (deciding between options).
- **Country** — primary target market; all articles serve US/UK/CA/EU unless noted.
- **Schema** — every article gets Article + Breadcrumb schema automatically; listed value is the *additional* schema.
- **Internal links** — link every cluster article to its siblings + its category page (the site also auto-links related posts).
- **Images** — replace palette SVGs with original/licensed photos as budget allows; every image needs descriptive alt text.
- Full H2 outlines for each *type* are in [CONTENT-TEMPLATES.md](CONTENT-TEMPLATES.md) — briefs below list only article-specific sections.

✅ = already built as a sample article in the codebase.
